CNN · XAI · HCAI
Grocery Image Classifier
+ Full XAI Suite
03
The challenge
Built a CNN that classifies grocery images into 4 categories (880 images scraped from Google Images). The interesting part: V2 outperformed V4 — Dropout that should help actually hurt on a small dataset. Then I applied five XAI methods (Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, Integrated Gradients, LIME, KernelSHAP) to understand why the model fails. A 50 percentage-point accuracy gap between best (Meat 95.7%) and worst (Fruits 45.5%) class was the key fairness finding. Finished with a real A/B usability study.
V2 best: 84.7% val accuracy · A/B test: significant difference on 3/5 questions
Python TensorFlow Grad-CAM / Grad-CAM++ LIME KernelSHAP Integrated Gradients xplique

ML · PostgreSQL · Figma
SafeRide · Road Risk
Prediction for ANWB
04
The challenge
ANWB wanted to know: can weather data predict road incident risk in Breda? I joined four datasets (ANWB incidents + three KNMI weather tables), engineered a composite risk score, and compared four models. KNN (k=11) was chosen for deployment — not for raw accuracy but because it maintained the best balance across all three risk classes. An important finding: upsampling the minority class hurt performance dramatically (43% vs 69%). Also designed the complete mobile app UI in Figma.
69.2% accuracy on 138K records · KNN selected for best class balance
